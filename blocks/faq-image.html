Common questions

Everything you need to know before joining the intensive.

Do I need painting experience?
No! The intensive is designed for all levels, from complete beginners to experienced painters.
What materials do I need?
Basic watercolor supplies. We'll send you a detailed materials list with budget-friendly options after registration.
Will sessions be recorded?
Yes, all sessions are recorded. You get lifetime access to rewatch at your own pace.
What if I miss a live session?
Recordings are available within hours. You can catch up anytime and still participate in discussions.
Is there a refund policy?
Full refund within 24 hours of purchase if you haven't accessed the materials.
