Course program

Day 1

Foundation: Water & Color

Understanding how water behaves on paper. Basic washes, color mixing, and finding your first brushstrokes. Pure discovery, no pressure.

2
Day 2

Technique: Light & Shadow

Layering, glazing, and creating depth. We paint a landscape from reference together, with Nicolás guiding every stroke in real time.

3
Day 3

Expression: Your Own Voice

Bringing it all together. You choose your subject and create your own composition. Personal feedback from Nicolás on your work.
