Course program

Day 1 — Foundation: Water & Color
4 hours

Understanding how water behaves on paper. Basic washes, color mixing, and finding your brushstrokes. We start with experiments — no pressure, pure discovery.

Day 2 — Technique: Light & Shadow
4 hours

Layering, glazing, and creating depth. We paint a landscape from reference together, with Nicolás guiding every stroke in real time.

Day 3 — Expression: Your Own Voice
4 hours

Bringing it all together. You choose your subject and create your own composition. Personal feedback from Nicolás on your completed work.
