Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any painting experience?
No prior experience is required. The intensive is designed for all levels — from complete beginners to experienced painters looking for fresh perspectives.
What materials do I need?
You'll receive a detailed materials list after registration. Basic watercolor supplies are sufficient — professional-grade materials are not required to participate.
Will sessions be recorded?
Yes! All sessions are recorded and you'll have lifetime access to the recordings, so you can rewatch and practice at your own pace.
What if I miss a live session?
No worries — recordings are available within hours after each session. You can catch up at any time and still participate in the community discussion.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes, we offer a full refund within 24 hours of purchase if you haven't accessed the course materials. Contact us at info@artefactoschool.online.
