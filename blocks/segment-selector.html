Подходит ли этот интенсив именно вам?

Inspired Beginners

You've always wanted to paint but never knew where to start. You're drawn to watercolor because it feels free and expressive.

This intensive is your perfect first step — no experience needed, just curiosity and a willingness to play with water and color.

Художник на плато

Вы уже не новичек и ищете свой стиль, рисуете несколько лет и устали от рутины, возможно, работы не плохие — но все одинаковые.

Иногда, вы видите, что результат предсказуем, когда видите работы мастера в голове вопрос: «КАК это делает?!»

Master Seekers

You have technical skill but crave deeper understanding. You want to learn from a master who sees watercolor differently.

Nicolás López's philosophy of "listening to the water" will transform how you approach every painting.

Creative Retirees

You finally have the time to pursue your creative dreams. Painting is your way to express yourself and connect with a community.

This intensive is designed for all levels — enjoy the process, not just the result.
